Roadmap to the Platinum — about 200 hours, one playthrough

Three stages, in order. Nothing is missable except the one trophy flagged below, and there is no mission replay and no New Game Plus — so take optional missions and character quests as they appear, and keep a manual save at the start of every chapter.

    ⚠ The one possibly missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude. In Chapter 1, the morning after Down to Earth, follow Lucia from the kitchen to the balcony and accept her meditation. It is gone after Chapter 1, and it is not confirmed yet whether the trophy needs it — do it anyway. Everything else can be done after the story. Cheats switch trophies and saving off.

    Sources & how this guide is written

    Names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation list, frozen 18 Nov 2026 and cross-checked against Exophase, PSNProfiles, GTABase, Output Lag and PowerPyx — all agree on all 55. Every guide is this entry’s own words; it draws on the sources named beside each trophy and never copies their text. Five hidden trophies have no confirmed unlock method at launch (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin); their guides say so plainly instead of guessing. Two more (Strangers & Freaks, Fair Game) are partly unconfirmed.
